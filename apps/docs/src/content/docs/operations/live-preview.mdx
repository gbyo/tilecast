Open a screen's **Overview** tab to use **Live preview**. Tilecast asks the Player for a still image and refreshes it about every 20 seconds while the page is open. Select **Refresh** to request another image.

## Choose the right view

| Feature                      | Best for                                             | Stored?                                         | What it tells you                                |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **Live preview**             | Checking a recent screenshot from the Player         | May be kept through preview or Snapshot history | What the Player captured recently                |
| **Watch live**               | Watching the Player while the live window is open    | No                                              | What the Player is capturing during that session |
| **Snapshot history**         | Looking back at saved screenshots                    | Yes, according to Snapshot history limits       | What the Player captured at those snapshot times |
| **Activity / Proof of Play** | Checking playback history and what Tilecast reported | Yes, according to Activity retention            | What the Player reported as playing              |

A screenshot or stream only shows what the Player captured at that moment. It does not prove that the correct content played for an entire scheduled window.

Activity / Proof of Play records what the Player reported as playing; it is not a recording of the screen.

## Watch the screen live

<Steps>

1. Open **Screens** > **Fleet** and select an online screen.
2. On **Overview**, select **Watch live** in **Live preview**.
3. Keep the **Live stream** window open while you watch.
4. Select **Stop watching** when you are finished.

</Steps>

Watch live is separate from the periodic preview. The stream runs only while the window is open, and closing it ends the session. Tilecast does not save the stream frames to previews, Activity, or backups.

If the stream connection ends, select **Retry stream** in the window to reconnect to the current session.

Watch live picks the best format that the Player, your browser, and the server all support. The target line in the window shows what it chose.

| Format | Size and rate                                                                           | When it is used                                                                                                |
| ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| H.264  | Up to 1280 × 720 at 30 frames per second, or 1280 × 720 at 15, or 960 × 540 at 15 or 10 | A Tilecast Edge Player with a working H.264 encoder and a browser that can decode the stream through WebCodecs |
| MJPEG  | 960 × 540 at 10, or 640 × 360 at 8                                                      | When the Player or browser cannot use H.264                                                                    |

H.264 needs a secure connection: open Studio over HTTPS (or on `localhost`). Over plain HTTP the browser cannot decode H.264, so Watch live uses MJPEG. If H.264 stops working in your browser, Watch live switches to MJPEG by itself.

The best format is 720p at 30 frames per second. Only an Edge Player with a hardware H.264 encoder that passes its startup test offers it. An Edge Player that encodes H.264 in software stays at 540p at 15 frames per second, so Watch live does not use much processor time on a Player without hardware encoding. There is no quality setting: Watch live chooses the best format for you.

These are ceilings, not guaranteed rates. A Player that passes the startup test can still be too slow in practice. If a Player cannot keep up, Watch live steps down one format for that session (720p30, then 720p15, then 540p15, then 540p10, then MJPEG). A 720p30 stream that stays well below 30 frames per second steps down to 720p15 because a steady 15 is better than an uneven 20. It never steps back up until you open a new session. Playback on the screen always comes first. A slow connection drops frames or briefly pauses and resumes at the next keyframe instead of building a backlog. H.264 targets about 2.5 Mbit/s at 720p30, 1.5 Mbit/s at 720p15, and 0.9 Mbit/s at 540p15; each MJPEG frame is capped at 100 KiB. Close Watch live when you are finished so the Player stops doing the capture work.

## When preview is unavailable

  src={screenshot}
  darkSrc={darkScreenshot}

/>

- **Watch live** works only while the screen is online.
- Protected Player screens are not captured. AirPlay-mirrored output is also excluded.
- On Android, opening a protected screen during capture can discard the frame. Capture resumes after the Player displays an eligible window again.
- An offline Player cannot send a new screenshot, so the last preview may be stale.
- A stale Player with no cached screenshot shows **Offline** in the preview panel. If a cached screenshot exists, it remains visible as stale.
- On the Screens grid, a thin line under each preview shows how current the image is. Nothing shows while the image is fresh (45 seconds or less). An amber line with a moving segment means the image is between 45 seconds and 2 minutes old and Tilecast is waiting for another capture. A still red line means the image is more than 2 minutes old, or the Player reported a capture error. The age badge and **Capture error** label say the same in text. The line does not move when the system or Studio is set to reduce motion, and it does not show for offline, disabled, or revoked Players or for protected Player screens.
- Some Player or device states do not allow capture. Studio shows the preview as unavailable or reports a capture error.

Live preview shows a recent image; it does not prove that scheduled content played.
