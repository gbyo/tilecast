title: View a live preview or stream
description: Check a Player's current image or watch its output while the stream window is open.

Open a screen's **Overview** tab to use **Live preview**. Tilecast requests a still image from the Player and refreshes it about every 20 seconds while the page is open. Select **Refresh** to request another image.

## Choose the right evidence

| Feature | Best for | Stored? | What it proves |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Live preview** | A recent still image from the Player | The current preview can be retained as part of normal preview/snapshot behavior | What the Player captured recently |
| **Watch live** | Watching the visible Player output while the dialog is open | No; stream frames stay in memory only | What the Player is capturing during that session |
| **Snapshot history** | Looking back at bounded periodic screenshots | Yes, according to Snapshot history limits | What the Player captured at those snapshot times |
| **Activity / Proof of Play** | Playback history and compliance | Yes, according to Activity retention | What the Player reported as playing |

A screenshot or stream is visual evidence, not proof that the correct content played for the full scheduled window. Proof of Play is Player-reported playback evidence, not a recording of the display.

## Watch the screen live

<Steps>

1. Open **Screens** > **Fleet** and select an online screen.
2. On **Overview**, select **Watch live** in **Live preview**.
3. Keep the **Live stream** window open while you watch.
4. Select **Stop watching** when you are finished.

</Steps>

The stream is separate from the periodic preview. It relays Player output only while the window is open; closing the window stops renewing the session. Tilecast does not save the stream frames to previews, Activity, or backups. Actual frame updates depend on the Player and network.

## When a view is unavailable

- **Watch live** is available only while the screen is online.
- A protected Player screen is not captured. AirPlay-mirrored output is also excluded.
- An offline Player cannot send a fresh image. The preview may be stale until it reconnects.
- Some Player or device states do not allow capture. Studio reports the preview as unavailable or shows a capture error.

Live preview shows a recent image; it does not prove that scheduled content played.
