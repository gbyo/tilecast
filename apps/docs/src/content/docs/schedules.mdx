Create a weekly schedule to show a Playlist or Layout at the same local time on selected days. For example, use one to play a lunch menu from Monday through Friday, 10:30 to 13:00.

The presentation chooser supports server search and **Load more**, and target selectors include screens and Display Groups beyond the first page. To find a schedule you already made, see [Find a schedule](#find-a-schedule).

## Before you start

- An **Owner** or **Administrator** account is needed to create or change schedules. Other roles can open a schedule and read its outcome, but not change it.
- Prepare a Playlist with content that is **Ready**, or publish the Layout you want to schedule. Tilecast won't save a schedule that references an empty Playlist, unready media, or an unpublished Layout.

## Create the schedule

The editor is one page that reads from top to bottom: what the schedule is, what it plays, when it runs, and where. A **Schedule outcome** pane beside it shows what your draft will do. Nothing is saved until you select **Create schedule**.

<Steps>

1. Open **Operations** > **Schedules**.
2. Select **Create schedule**.
3. Enter a **Name**.
4. Leave **Enabled** on. The switch takes effect when you save, not before.
5. Under **Presentation**, choose **Content**.
6. Select **Choose a Playlist or Layout**, then pick one.
7. Select **Use this presentation**.
8. Under **Timing**, choose **Weekly**.
9. Select the days the schedule should run.
10. Enter the **Starts** and **Ends** times.
11. Check the **Timezone**. It starts as your organization's default. Search by city or region to choose another.
12. Under **Targets**, select **Add targets**.
13. Search for screens and Display Groups, then choose one or more. Each target appears as a row you can remove.
14. Select **Create schedule**. You stay in the editor, now showing the saved schedule.

</Steps>

Select **Limit to a date range** under **Timing** if the rule should start or stop on specific dates. Without one, the rule repeats every selected weekday.

Choose **One-time** instead of **Weekly** for a single event. Enter the start and end in the schedule's timezone, not your browser's.

## Read the outcome

The **Schedule outcome** pane answers four questions about your draft: what it shows, when it runs, where it applies, and at which priority. On a tablet or phone, select **Review** in the header to open the same pane. A number on **Review** counts things that need your attention.

Below those answers, the **Next-run check** asks the server what would happen at the schedule's next run. It looks at every screen you targeted, including every screen in a Display Group, and tells you on how many screens this schedule would play. If another schedule would win on some screens, the check names it and says why, for example that it has a higher priority.

The check covers the next run only. A later run can differ if another schedule applies at a different time. Treat it as a way to catch problems, not as a promise.

While you edit, the pane keeps the last answer and marks it **Checking…** until a new one arrives. If the check fails, you can still save. If it finds screens that can't run display control, fix that first: those screens block saving.

## Control which schedule wins

Open **Conflict handling** only when this schedule shares screens with another one. A higher priority is preferred when active schedules overlap on a screen. Choose **Important** or **Special event** when this schedule should take precedence, or **Custom** to enter a number from -999 to 999. When priorities match, the schedule that targets a screen directly wins, then the one that started later. The next-run check shows the result.

In the target picker, a screen that belongs to a Display Group is offered as that group. Choosing the group schedules all its members together. Outside an active schedule window, the assigned fallback content continues to play.

## Time and playback behavior

Weekly times use the selected timezone, including daylight-saving changes. If the **Ends** time is earlier than the **Starts** time, playback ends the following day. Equal start and end times make a 24-hour window.

Players evaluate timing locally from the last schedule manifest they received. During a server or network outage, they can continue switching at schedule boundaries already in that manifest, but they can't receive schedule or content changes until they reconnect. Playback during an outage still depends on the needed content being available on the Player; content set to stream or not yet downloaded needs a network connection.

To remove a schedule, open **More actions** in the header and choose **Delete schedule**. Its screens then use another applicable schedule, or their assigned fallback content.

## Find a schedule

**Operations** > **Schedules** lists every schedule with the count at the top. Each row answers what the schedule does, when it runs, and where:

- **Schedule**: the name, which opens the schedule, and its description.
- **Status**: **Enabled** or **Disabled**. This is the saved setting. It doesn't say whether the schedule is playing right now.
- **When**: the days and hours, then any date range and the schedule's timezone. A window that ends the next day says so.
- **Presentation**: the Playlist or Layout it shows, or the Display Control action it runs.
- **Targets**: the first two screens or Display Groups, then a count such as **+3**. Hover or focus the count to see them all.
- **Priority**: the exact number. A higher priority wins when eligible schedules overlap.
- **Updated**: when the schedule last changed.

Narrow screens drop the less important columns, and phones show a list instead of a table.

Search matches schedule names. **Filters** narrows by **Status**, **Schedule type** (weekly or one-time), and **Presentation** (Playlist, Layout, or Display control). Active filters appear as chips under the toolbar. **Clear filters** removes them but keeps your search and sort. Use the sort menu to order by **Recently updated**, **Name**, or **Highest priority**.

Search, filters, and sort run on the server, so they cover every schedule and not only the ones loaded so far. The list loads up to 100 schedules at a time; select **Load more** for the rest.

The **⋯** menu on each row opens the schedule. **Owner** and **Administrator** accounts can also delete it there after confirming. Other roles can open a schedule but not change it.
