---
title: Content submissions
description: Review the exact Playlist, Layout, or Campaign snapshot that an author submitted for publication.
---

Use **Content review** > **Open submission inbox** for explicit Playlist, Layout, and Campaign submissions.

Submitting creates an exact snapshot for review. The author can continue editing their private draft, but those later edits do not silently change what the reviewer is approving.

## Review workflow

1. Open the submitted item.
2. Review the exact submitted snapshot.
3. Choose **Approve** or **Request changes**.
4. A request for changes requires a reason for the author.
5. After approval, publish immediately or schedule the approved snapshot when your role allows it.

Organization policy under **Settings** > **Content review** controls which direct publication actions require review, whether self-approval is allowed, and whether approval automatically publishes.

Campaign publication remains restricted to the roles allowed by Campaign permissions.

This workflow is separate from [Form approvals](../forms-approvals/), which reviews submitted Form records rather than presentation content.
