Tilecast Studio is the browser app for your installation. Open your Tilecast Server address in a browser and sign in. Use the guides here to create content and decide when and where it appears.

## Start with a display

A screen needs a paired Player before it can show anything. If you are setting up a new screen, first [install and pair a Player](../players/install-and-pair/). Use [Display Groups](../screens/display-groups/) when screens should share assignments or playback behavior.

## Check the result

Use [Activity](../operations/activity/) to check playback the Player confirmed, screen health, and incidents. [Screen status](../operations/screen-status/) explains what Fleet's connection labels mean, and [Explain a Screen's playback selection](../screens/explain-playback/) shows why a screen plays what it does. For account, security, backup, or Player policy settings, see [Manage Tilecast](../manage/). To connect another system, see [Automate and extend](../integrations/).
