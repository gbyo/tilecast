Use these pages to manage Studio accounts and installation-wide settings. Organization controls live under **Settings**; each person manages their own factors under **My Account**. Backup management is Owner-only; other pages note role requirements.

## Accounts and security

- [Manage users and roles](./users-and-roles/) to create accounts, limit screen access, or suspend an account.
- [Protect Studio accounts](./sign-in-security/) to enroll a second factor or require one for some or all accounts.

## Player behavior and recovery

- [Set Player policies](./player-policies/) to set organization defaults or override them for a Display Group or screen.
- [Back up and restore an installation](./backups/) to protect database, media, and cached Player releases.
- [Update Tilecast Player](./player-updates/) to verify signed releases and deploy them to selected screens.

Screen enrollment and groups are covered in [pair or replace a screen](../screens/pair-and-replace/) and [manage Display Groups](../screens/display-groups/).
