---
title: Active hours and power
description: Define when normal signage should run and understand Android Power Assist versus Linux Display Control.
---

Open **Settings** > **Active hours and power** for organization defaults, or override them for a Display Group or screen.

## Active hours

Active hours use an IANA timezone, selected weekdays, and local start/end times. An end time at or before the start creates an overnight window that belongs to the selected start day.

Tilecast evaluates timezone rules rather than assuming every day is exactly 24 hours, so daylight-saving gaps and overlaps are handled as calendar boundaries.

Outside active hours, ordinary playback can move to the configured off-hours state. A Takeover can still outrank that state when urgent content must be shown.

## Power is platform-specific

Android **Power Assist** uses bounded Android sleep/wake capabilities where the device supports them. A policy request is not proof that the connected TV physically changed power state.

Linux uses separate capability-reported [Display Control](../../screens/display-control/) for HDMI-CEC/DDC actions. Linux does not reinterpret Android-only power-assist settings such as sleep outside active hours as an operating-system sleep command.

Test the exact Player and panel combination before relying on unattended power behavior.
