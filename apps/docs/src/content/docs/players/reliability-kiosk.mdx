Open **Settings** > **Reliability and kiosk** for organization defaults. A Display Group or screen can override supported values through its Player policy.

## Health means playback progress

Tilecast distinguishes:

1. the Player process is alive;
2. the renderer responds;
3. playback is meaningfully progressing.

Only the third is enough to call playback healthy. A frozen video can have a responsive process, while a long-lived still image can be perfectly healthy without changing pixels.

Players use content-aware progress evidence and a bounded recovery ladder. Repeated failure enters safe mode instead of restart-looping.

## Platform behavior

Android offers **Standard Reliability** on a normally installed Player and can use **Managed Kiosk** only when Android confirms the required device-owner/lock-task capability. Selecting the policy is not proof that the device is provisioned.

Linux exposes its own kiosk/fullscreen and display-sleep behavior. Boot startup, desktop/session lockdown, and process lifecycle still depend on the installed Linux service and host environment.

Platform-specific Android controls do not become active on Linux simply because they share a policy document.

## Recovery

Recovery can retry content, recreate rendering state, restart bounded Player components, and finally enter safe mode. Cached playback and the server relationship are preserved where possible.

Use [Activity](../../operations/activity/) to see the evidence and recovery events a Player actually reported.
