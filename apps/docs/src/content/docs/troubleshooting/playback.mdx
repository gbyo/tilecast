---
title: Playback and schedules
description: Diagnose connected Players that show the wrong content, no content, stale content, or a schedule at the wrong time.
---

## The Player is online but content is wrong

Start with the screen detail page and **Activity**. Compare what Tilecast selected with what the Player reported as playing.

Tilecast can select temporary content ahead of the normal assignment. The order is:

1. active Takeover;
2. on stable Linux, active AirPlay;
3. Quick Present;
4. the highest-priority active Schedule;
5. the normal screen or Display Group assignment.

When the temporary item ends, Tilecast evaluates the current schedule and fallback again.

## The screen shows no content

Check whether the screen actually has an assignment and whether every referenced item is currently playable.

A connected Player can keep the previous committed presentation when a newer one cannot be prepared. Media that is not Ready, expired content, an unavailable Website, a missing capability, or an incompatible Layout can prevent part or all of a presentation from becoming active.

Use the [Dependency Graph](../../administration/dependency-graph/) when you need to trace the assets and Data Sources behind the presentation.

## A schedule changes at the wrong time

Check the Schedule's IANA timezone and the Player clock warning. Tilecast uses server-corrected Player time for schedules, availability windows, temporary presentations, and offline evaluation.

Around daylight-saving changes, calendar rules are evaluated in the configured timezone rather than assuming every day has 24 hours.

A future one-time Schedule created after a Player goes offline cannot activate until that Player receives a newer manifest. Recurring rules already present in the committed manifest can continue to evaluate offline when their required content is cached.

## Cached content disappeared

Player cache and prepared state are bound to the Tilecast installation and logical screen. A confirmed server change, pairing replacement, or other identity change can invalidate state that no longer belongs to that relationship.

Do not copy another screen's cache or Player state directory as a recovery method.
