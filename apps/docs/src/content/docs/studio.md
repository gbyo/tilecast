title: Tilecast Studio

Tilecast Studio is the browser app for managing your installation. Open your Tilecast Server address in a browser and sign in.

## Start with a display

If you are setting up a new screen, first [install a Player](../players/) and [pair the display](../players/pair-a-display/). Use [Screens > Display Groups](../screens/display-groups/) when screens should share assignments or playback behavior.

## Create something to play

- [Upload and organize media](../media/) for images and videos.
- [Add website content](../website-content/) when a display needs a public website.
- [Connect a Data Source](../data-sources/) when content needs values from a feed, file, calendar, or Manual Table.
- [Create and reuse Widgets](../widgets/) to show clocks, notices, or data.
- [Build a Playlist](../playlists/) to choose fullscreen content in order.
- [Build a Layout](../layouts/) to arrange several areas on one screen.
- [Coordinate a release with a Campaign](../campaigns/) to publish related schedule changes together.
- [Review content before publication](./content-review/) when Playlists, Layouts, or Campaigns need approval.
- [Use the content submission inbox](./content-submissions/) to review the exact snapshot an author submitted.
- [Build Forms](./forms/) to collect structured submissions that can feed signage.
- [Review Form records](./forms-approvals/) from the central Approvals inbox.

## Assign, schedule, and monitor

Use [Schedules](../schedules/) for a recurring or one-time playback window. Use [Activity](../operations/activity/) to check playback the Player confirmed, screen health, and incidents. [Screen status](../operations/screen-status/) explains what Fleet's connection labels mean.

For account, security, backup, or Player policy settings, see [Administration](../administration/). To send notifications or connect another system, see [Integrations](../integrations/).
