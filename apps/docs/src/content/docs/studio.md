Tilecast Studio is where you manage everything that appears on your displays. Open it in a browser at your server's address. There's nothing to install.

Studio is available in English, Spanish, and Russian.

## Where things are

The Studio sidebar groups its pages by the job you're doing:

| Section           | Pages                                     | Use it to                                                                                    |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Screens**       | **Fleet**, **Display Groups**             | Pair displays, check their status, and group screens that share content.                     |
| **Content**       | **Media**, **Widgets**, **Data Sources**  | Upload images and videos, and build content such as clocks, menus, and feeds.                |
| **Presentations** | **Playlists**, **Layouts**, **Campaigns** | Put content in order, arrange it in zones on the screen, or plan a run of scheduled content. |
| **Operations**    | **Schedules**, **Plugins**                | Decide when content plays, and add optional built-in features.                               |

**Activity** and **Settings** are near the bottom of the sidebar. **Activity** shows what Players confirm they displayed, screen health, and the audit log. **Settings** holds organization, user, and Player options.

## Key terms

- **Screen:** one paired display. The server works out its status: online, recently online, stale, offline, disabled, or revoked.
- **Display Group:** a set of screens that share fallback content, schedules, and playback position.
- **Fallback content:** what a screen or Display Group plays when no schedule or takeover applies.
- **Takeover:** a temporary override that replaces scheduled and fallback content on the screens it targets, for example during an emergency.

## Who can do what

Every Studio account has one role:

- **Owner** has full control, including organization-wide imports and exports and adding new Player releases.
- **Administrator** runs the installation day to day: screens, schedules, takeovers, settings, and users.
- **Editor** creates and publishes content, playlists, and layouts.
- **Contributor** drafts content and submits it for review, but can't publish it or assign it to screens.
- **Viewer** can see Studio and screen status but can't change content or settings.

Only an Owner or an Administrator can approve a pairing request or change a device's credential.
