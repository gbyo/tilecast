---
title: Locations
description: Organize screens with reusable, region-neutral place information.
---

Use **Settings** > **Locations** to create places that can be referenced by screens and location-aware targeting.

Location address fields are optional and region-neutral. Tilecast does not require a U.S.-style city/state/ZIP shape; locality, administrative area, and postal code are stored independently so installations outside the United States do not have to force their addresses into an American format.

Locations can also be useful as a targeting scope for features such as built-in plugins. A location is organization metadata, not a network boundary: putting two screens in the same location does not imply that they share a VLAN, subnet, or Player capability.

Review each screen's location from its screen details when you move hardware between buildings or rooms.
