Use **Settings** > **General** for installation-wide identity and regional behavior. These values apply to organization-owned signage and data, not just the browser you are using.

## Regional settings

Tilecast stores a canonical locale and IANA timezone for organization-level formatting. You can also choose date and time formats and the first day of the week.

The first day of week can follow the locale's CLDR convention or use an explicit weekday. Clock, Date, and World Clock Widgets can inherit these organization choices when their own format or timezone is left at the organization default.

Your personal Studio language is separate. Changing **My Account** preferences does not silently change signage formatting for everyone.

## Organization identity

Set the organization name and optional short name here. Other organization-facing settings such as branding and locations have their own sections.
