---
title: Content and playback settings
description: Set organization defaults for playback, media, websites, and scheduling.
---

Tilecast keeps organization-wide content defaults under **Settings**. These defaults do not replace the values authored directly on a Playlist item or Website; they provide the effective Player policy when content delegates to Player defaults.

## Playback

Playback defaults include volume, fit mode, default image duration, transition, audio enabled, and whether supported content resumes after a Player restart.

A Playlist item with **Use Player defaults** delegates those values to the effective Player policy. An item with its own authored values keeps them.

## Websites

Website policy controls the runtime behavior Tilecast applies around remote content, including timeout and cookie/storage behavior. A startup-only clear setting is evaluated when the Player process starts; changing that setting later does not unexpectedly erase a currently playing website mid-session.

## Media and scheduling

Media settings control organization defaults and bounds around stored or prepared media. Scheduling behavior continues to come from each Schedule's typed dates, times, timezone, priority, and targets; Tilecast does not expose hidden scheduling switches that have no runtime effect.

## Overrides

Player-related values follow Tilecast's policy order:

1. screen override;
2. Display Group policy;
3. organization default;
4. built-in default.

See [Set Player policies](../player-policies/) for group and screen overrides.
