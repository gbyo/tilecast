---
title: My Account and preferences
description: Manage your own Tilecast Studio profile, appearance, language, and sign-in security.
---

Open **My Account** from the account menu for settings that belong to you rather than the whole Tilecast installation.

Your personal preferences can include Studio language, appearance, and interface density. They do not change the organization's signage locale, timezone, or Player policy.

Sign-in security also lives here. Every account can enroll its own supported second factors; an organization-wide MFA requirement under **Settings** controls who must enroll one.

For roles and screen scope, see [Users and roles](../users-and-roles/). For organization-wide sign-in policy, see [Sign-in security](../sign-in-security/).
