---
title: My Account and preferences
description: Change your own Studio language, appearance, and sign-in security.
---

Open **My Account** from the account menu to change settings that belong only to your account.

You can change preferences such as Studio language, appearance, and interface density. These do not change the organization's timezone, signage formatting, or Player settings.

You can also add or remove supported second-factor sign-in methods here. If the organization requires MFA, **Settings** controls who must enroll one, while each person manages their own factors in **My Account**.

## Tokens and CLI sign-ins

**Sign-in security** also lists the credentials that act as your account outside the browser:

- **Authorized operators** lists each `tilecast` CLI sign-in you approved. Select **Revoke** to end one immediately. See [Manage Tilecast from the command line](../../integrations/cli/).
- **Personal access tokens** creates and revokes expiring tokens for scripts, CI jobs, and the CLI. See [Create a personal access token](../../integrations/personal-access-tokens/).

These credentials belong to you and follow your current role. They are separate from the Owner's [integration tokens](../../integrations/tokens/).

For permissions and screen access, see [Users and roles](../users-and-roles/). For organization-wide MFA settings, see [Sign-in security](../sign-in-security/).
